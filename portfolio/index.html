Leadership Case Studies

Short briefs behind the claims in my resume: the problem, what I did, and what changed.

Volvo — Three EPA27 Programs (Cross-Regional Delivery) ~110 people
Situation In 2024, three EPA27 programs across the US, Europe, and India (about 110 people, 45+ ECUs per program plus supplier ECUs) were each tracking status their own way, with no single view across all three.
Action Aligned the teams region by region, one-on-one and on-site in Sweden, instead of by mandate. Built one program-to-ECU tracker (SharePoint, Power BI, Python) so everyone worked from the same picture.
Impact All three programs are now ready for start of production. The tracker became a reference model, and I was asked to teach the approach to other system architects.
Signal Cross-regional execution through alignment, not authority.
Volvo — New BEV Program from Pre-Study Pending SOP
Situation A new battery-electric truck program started with no project manager and open questions on scope and competitiveness.
Action Led the pre-study (feasibility, technology readiness, program risk, trade-offs), took part in every strategy discussion, and negotiated with brand and other stakeholders to keep the product competitive.
Impact The program is pending start of production. Of the three large and numerous smaller pre-studies I have led, all others are implemented.
Signal Ownership from a blank page.
Volvo — Production BEV Quality Support Resolved on time
Situation Customer product and quality issues on the in-production BEV truck needed a new owner and a reliable handling process.
Action Took it over immediately and built the documentation and the issue-handling process.
Impact All quality issues since have been resolved on time.
Signal Steadying a live product.
Danfoss — Contested Forklift Bid (Patented Controls) $10M award
Situation A Hyster-Yale forklift program was contested by Bosch Rexroth, Parker Hannifin, and Eaton. Standard compensator-based hydraulics cost too much for a safety-critical lift-under-load function.
Action Replaced the mechanical compensator with pressure-sensor-based control logic and proved it on a test forklift in about three months.
Impact Won the program: a $10M customer award for Danfoss, a granted US patent, and the 2017 Technical and Service Excellence Award.
Signal Software in place of costly hardware, under competition.
Omni Powertrain — US eMobility Team from Scratch 1 → 4 in a year
Situation Omni had no US electrical engineering team and no recruiting support.
Action As the first US electrical engineer, sourced, screened, and hired three engineers while supporting live customer programs; moved tracking from Excel to Agile and Azure DevOps.
Impact The team helped a commercial EV truck OEM integrate Omni's eAxle into its first vehicle platform.
Signal Building capability while delivering.
Halliburton — Simulation-Based Testing HIL + simulation
Situation Control code for frac surface equipment (engines, transmissions, pumps, blenders) could only be tested on costly field machines.
Action Built hardware-in-the-loop and predictive simulation models on National Instruments hardware, validated against field data.
Impact Control code could be tested without renting field equipment, with the potential to save millions of dollars in field testing.
Signal Simulation in place of expensive physical testing.
